# Phishing Email Detector

## Dataset

The datasets I have used for this project are the Nazario Phishing Corpus, which provides phishing emails, and the SpamAssassin easy_ham corpus for legitimate emails.

I chose these datasets as they are publically available, contain real emails and are small enough to locally process on my computer without the need of specialised hardware.

A limitation of these datasets is that they are relatively old and come from different sources, therefore the model may not perform as well on newer and differently formatted emails.
